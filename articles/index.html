More than a year of turnover, that’s massive! Guidewire’s $1.7B bet on Hyperexponential

Guidewire just announced the largest acquisition ever in the European InsurTech scene, and unveiled its new platform approach to agentic insurance at Connections Las Vegas.

October 28, 2026·4 min read· ALBy Ahmed LIMAM

It took less than three minutes on stage. Mike Rosenbaum, Guidewire CEO, flashed the logo lockup — GUIDEWIRE + hyperexponential — and Fontainebleau’s ballroom went silent before erupting. For anyone tracking specialty insurance infrastructure, the number is staggering.

At $1.7 billion upfront, plus a $400M earnout, this isn’t just Guidewire’s biggest deal. It’s the biggest exit ever for a European InsurTech, eclipsing everything Wefox, Zego, or Marshmallow ever raised. And it signals a hard pivot: from core systems vendor to agentic platform orchestrator.

01 / The Numbers

From $90M raised to a $1.7B exit

Hyperexponential’s story is a masterclass in efficient capital. Founded in 2017 by Amrit Santhirasenan and Michael Johnson — both ex-Applied AI at Cambridge — the London-based team raised just $90M total before this exit. That includes a $73M Series B in 2023 led by Battery Ventures.

In 2024, the company reportedly did north of $35M ARR, growing 100% YoY, powering pricing decisions for some of the most complex risks on the planet. Its hx Renew platform became the quiet standard for specialty and commercial underwriters who couldn’t bend Excel or legacy pricing engines to model real risk.

02 / Platform Doctrine

Not just an acquisition: a new platform doctrine

The second half of Rosenbaum’s keynote mattered more than the price tag. Guidewire unveiled what it calls Agentic Insurance — a re-architecture of InsuranceSuite around autonomous AI agents that can quote, underwrite, and service without human-in-the-loop for standard risks, while escalating complex decisions to hx-powered pricing intelligence.

“We are moving beyond the system of record. InsuranceSuite becomes the system of action — where pricing models from Hyperexponential become callable tools for agents that act on behalf of the carrier.”

MRMike Rosenbaum, CEO — GuidewireConnections Keynote, 2026

What this unlocks in practice:

  • ✓

    hx as the pricing brain

    hx Renew models exposed as APIs / MCP tools for Guidewire’s new Agent Framework. No more batch imports.

  • ✓

    Specialty-first agents

    First agents target commercial lines pricing, submission ingestion, and renewal triage — where $75B premium complexity lives.

  • ✓

    Data flywheel

    Every quote and bind feeds back into hx loss cost models. Guidewire Cloud customers opt-in to anonymized improvement loop.

“We built hx to kill the spreadsheet tax in specialty. Guidewire gives us the distribution to kill it at global scale.”

ASAmrit Santhirasenan, CEO — Hyperexponential
03 / Implications

Why this is a game changer

Three reasons this deal redraws the map for InsurTech and core systems alike:

01

European validation at scale

After years of ‘US acquires EU for talent’, this is pure IP acquisition at unicorn+ pricing. London pricing excellence is now priced like Silicon Valley AI infra.

02

The end of best-of-breed pricing?

If Guidewire bundles hx natively, standalone pricing tools (Akur8, Earnix, hyperexponential competitors) face a platform vs point solution fight inside InsuranceSuite.

03

Agentic is the new cloud

Like Guidewire Cloud in 2018, agentic becomes the mandatory upgrade path. Carriers who don’t adopt agent frameworks risk being priced out — literally.

The open question: will Guidewire keep hx as a neutral platform for non-Guidewire carriers, or will it become the exclusive intelligence layer for InsuranceSuite? Rosenbaum hinted at the former on stage, but the incentives point to the latter. With $1.2B in revenue to protect and grow, bundling is inevitable.

For Tunisia and MENA, the signal is equally loud. Specialty pricing — marine, energy, political risk — is exactly where regional carriers compete globally. If agentic pricing becomes table stakes in London and Bermuda, North African insurers plugging into Guidewire Cloud will inherit that capability overnight.

This is just the start of the agentic stack. Pricing first, claims next.
